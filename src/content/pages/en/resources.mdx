## Free Resource Downloads

Resources on this page are free for:

- **Families and caregivers** — caring for elderly with dysphagia at home
- **Care home staff** — care workers, nurses, kitchen staff
- **Healthcare professionals** — speech therapists, occupational therapists, dietitians
- **Chefs and kitchen managers** — preparing IDDSI-compliant meals
- **Students and researchers** — reference materials for related disciplines

All materials may be freely downloaded, printed, and shared for non-commercial use. For institutional training or publication use, please contact us first.

---

## IDDSI Reference Charts

**IDDSI 8-Level Chart (Traditional Chinese)**
- Format: A4 PDF, printable
- Content: All 8 levels (Level 0–7+EC), colour-coded, food examples per level, dangerous foods list
- Ideal for: Posting in kitchen or nursing station

**IDDSI Family Reference Card**
- Format: A5 PDF (double-sided)
- Content: Simplified 8-level guide for non-specialists
- Ideal for: Posting on the refrigerator door

**Fork Pressure Test & Syringe Flow Test Instructions**
- Format: A4 PDF
- Content: Step-by-step illustrated instructions, pass criteria
- Ideal for: Kitchen quality control

## Recipes

**Soft Meal Recipe Collection (Level 4–5) Volume 1**
- Format: A4 PDF, full colour
- Content: 5 recipes (Steamed Egg with Silken Tofu, Pumpkin Cream Purée, Smooth Chicken Congee, Steamed Fish with Broccoli, Banana Oat Porridge)
- Each recipe includes: ingredients, steps, texture test results, nutrition tips
- Ideal for: Home and small care home kitchens

## Care Home Management Forms

**Meal Observation Record Form**
- Format: A4 PDF, handwrite-friendly
- Content: Per-meal eating records (amount eaten, choking incidents, food texture, abnormalities)
- Columns: 7-day recording capacity
- Ideal for: Providing to speech therapists for follow-up

**Pre-meal Checklist for Dysphagia Patients**
- Format: A5 PDF (single page)
- Content: 10-point pre-meal checklist (posture, oral hygiene, thickener preparation, texture verification)
- Ideal for: Daily use by care workers

---

## Guides and Information Booklets

**Understanding GBA Care Food Standard T/SATA 084-2025**
- Format: A4 PDF, 4 pages
- Content: Standard overview, comparison with IDDSI, applicable institutions, FAQ
- Ideal for: Care home management, procurement staff

**Hong Kong Dysphagia Resources Overview**
- Format: A4 PDF, 2 pages
- Content: Public hospital speech therapy department contacts, community resources, private speech therapist association
- Ideal for: Family referral information
