## Care Food Resource Centre

Resources on this page are free for:

- **Families and caregivers** — caring for elderly with dysphagia at home
- **Care home staff** — care workers, nurses, kitchen staff
- **Healthcare professionals** — speech therapists, occupational therapists, dietitians
- **Chefs and kitchen managers** — preparing IDDSI-compliant meals
- **Students and researchers** — reference materials for related disciplines

All materials may be freely downloaded, printed, and shared for non-commercial use. For institutional training or publication use, please contact us first.

## Free Downloads: Official IDDSI Resources

The following resources are provided free of charge by **IDDSI (iddsi.net)** and can be downloaded directly:

**IDDSI Complete Framework Document**
- Source: [iddsi.net/Framework](https://www.iddsi.net/Framework)
- Content: Full definitions for all 8 levels, food testing methods, drink thickness testing methods
- Language: English (official); multilingual versions including Traditional Chinese available
- Ideal for: Healthcare professionals, care home training materials

**IDDSI Quick Reference Sheet**
- Source: [iddsi.net/resources](https://www.iddsi.net/resources)
- Content: A4 colour chart, printable for kitchen or nursing station
- Ideal for: Daily use by kitchen and care staff

**IDDSI Testing Cards**
- Source: [iddsi.net/resources](https://www.iddsi.net/resources)
- Content: Fork pressure test and syringe flow test instruction cards
- Ideal for: Kitchen quality control and care home self-audit

**IDDSI e-Learning**
- Source: [iddsi.net/Training](https://www.iddsi.net/Training)
- Content: Free self-paced online course covering IDDSI framework application and texture characteristics per level
- Ideal for: All care food staff, particularly useful for onboarding new employees

> All links above point to the official IDDSI website. softmeal.org does not modify or endorse their content.

## Hong Kong Local Resources

### Hospital Authority (HA) Guidelines

**HA Patient Education Materials**
- Website: [ha.org.hk](https://www.ha.org.hk)
- Speech therapy departments at public hospitals across Hong Kong provide dysphagia assessment and dietary adjustment recommendations. Patient education leaflets are available from each hospital cluster.

**Community Geriatric Assessment Teams (CGAT)**
- Referral: via GP or social worker to HA Geriatric Day Hospitals
- Services: swallowing assessment, dietary advice, follow-up care

**Geriatric Specialist Outpatient Clinics**
- Referral: via GP
- Services: swallowing function assessment, speech therapy referral

### HKCSS Care Food Resources

**Hong Kong Council of Social Service (HKCSS)**
- Website: [hkcss.org.hk](https://www.hkcss.org.hk)
- HKCSS publishes care food guidelines and maintains a list of endorsed care food suppliers (Care Food Guide)
- Care homes may reference the HKCSS endorsed supplier list when making procurement decisions

## Professional Organisations

### Hong Kong Speech and Hearing Association (HKSHA)

**Hong Kong Speech and Hearing Association (HKSHA)**
- Website: [hksha.org](https://www.hksha.org)
- Functions: Maintains a directory of accredited speech therapists, publishes clinical guidelines, offers continuing education
- To find a private speech therapist, use the association's therapist search function
- Also publishes public education materials on dysphagia

### Hong Kong Association of Speech-Language Pathologists (HKSAT)

- Website: [hksat.org](https://www.hksat.org)
- Provides a register of registered speech-language pathologists, searchable by district

### Hong Kong Dietitians Association (HKDA)

**Hong Kong Dietitians Association (HKDA)**
- Website: [hkda.com.hk](https://www.hkda.com.hk)
- Functions: Maintains a directory of accredited dietitians
- Note: Dysphagia management in care home residents commonly requires joint input from speech therapists and dietitians, as malnutrition is a co-occurring concern. Both professionals should be involved in care planning where possible.
