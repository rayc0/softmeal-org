## Why Measuring Accuracy Matters

Thickener dosing errors directly affect whether a drink reaches the IDDSI level prescribed by the speech and language therapist (SLT). With some xanthan gum thickeners, a 1–2 gram difference can shift the result by a full IDDSI level.

The most common error among Hong Kong home caregivers is using visual estimation or ordinary teaspoons. Teaspoon volumes vary significantly by brand, and the difference between a heaped and a level teaspoon can exceed 50% of the intended dose.

---

## The Standard Preparation Sequence

SLTs typically prescribe thickener doses by **weight (grams)** rather than volume, as weight measurement is independent of how the scoop is held. The recommended preparation sequence is:

1. Measure the drink volume (typically 100–200ml)
2. **Weigh the required amount of thickener** using a digital scale (0.1g precision recommended)
3. Add the thickener in stages while stirring continuously
4. Wait 1–3 minutes for full dissolution and stabilisation
5. Verify consistency using the IDDSI Syringe Flow Test

## Essential Preparation Tools

### 1. Digital Kitchen Scale (0.1g Precision)

Standard kitchen scales (1–5g precision) are too imprecise for thickener measurement. Use a **jewellery-grade or precision kitchen scale accurate to 0.1g** for small-dose measurements.

**Buying in HK:**
- Ladies' Market area (Tung Choi Street, Mong Kok) and electronics shops on Sai Yeung Choi Street South carry 0.1g precision scales
- Online platforms (Taobao HK, Amazon) also stock precision scales
- Approximate price: HKD $80–$200 for 0.1g-accurate models

**Usage tips:**
- Use the Tare function to zero the scale with a small dish before measuring
- Do not measure directly onto the scale platform — powder residue is difficult to clean and can affect future readings

### 2. Graduated Measuring Cup

Accurate drink volume measurement is the first step. Use a clear graduated cup with markings at minimum 10–25ml intervals.

**Notes:**
- Read the meniscus at eye level to avoid parallax error
- Some thickener brands give dosing in grams per 100ml of drink, so accurate volume measurement is important

**Approximate price:** HKD $20–$80

### 3. Brand-Specific Measuring Scoops

Most commercial thickeners include a proprietary scoop. However, scoop volumes can vary between batches, and the heaped vs. level difference can account for 30–50% variation in actual weight delivered.

**Recommendation:**
- If the SLT recommends dosing by scoop, confirm whether "level" or "heaped" is intended
- Periodically weigh the scoop to verify it matches the gram target, and record the result

### 4. 10ml Syringe for IDDSI Flow Testing

The IDDSI Syringe Flow Test is the standard method for verifying drink consistency at home. It uses a 10ml syringe (needle removed) to measure how much liquid flows out over 10 seconds.

**Equipment needed:**
- 10ml needleless syringe
- Timer (accurate to seconds)
- IDDSI flow test reference card (free download at iddsi.net)

**Buying in HK:**
- Available at Watsons, Mannings, or independent pharmacies in Mong Kok
- Approximately HKD $3–$8 per syringe; can be cleaned and reused

### 5. Stirring Tools

Thorough stirring ensures the thickener dissolves evenly. Undissolved clumps alter texture and create an inconsistent consistency throughout the drink.

**Recommended tools:**
- **Manual stirring rod**: Suitable for most xanthan gum thickeners; stir clockwise for approximately 30–60 seconds
- **Small electric milk frother**: Speeds up dissolution but can introduce air bubbles in some thickeners — test with the specific brand first
- **Avoid forks**: Insufficient mixing surface; thickener tends to clump between tines

## Common Measuring Errors and Solutions

| Common Error | Consequence | Solution |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Using a regular teaspoon by eye | Dose varies every time | Switch to digital scale by weight |
| Inconsistent scoop technique | Same scoop = 30–50% volume variation | Standardise to "level scoop"; verify with scale |
| Testing immediately after mixing | Thickener not yet dissolved; reads thinner | Wait 1–3 minutes before testing |
| Testing hot drinks immediately | Starch-based thickeners continue thickening; over-thick result | Let hot drinks cool slightly before testing; prefer xanthan gum thickeners for hot drinks |
| Different people prepare differently | No standardisation across caregivers | Create a written prep chart and post it in the kitchen |

## Creating a Household Standard Protocol

For each family member with dysphagia, prepare a written preparation guide that includes:

- **Patient name and IDDSI level**
- **List of common drinks with thickener dose in grams for each**
- **Preparation steps and waiting time**
- **Syringe flow test result log** (record each preparation to track consistency over time)

This document can be updated at each SLT review appointment and shared with other family members or a domestic helper who assists with meal preparation.
