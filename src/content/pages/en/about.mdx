# Who We Are

**softmeal.org** is the open clinical-content imprint of **Carewells Limited** — operator of [CareEZ](https://careez.org) (point-of-care swallowing safety AI), SeniorDeli (IDDSI-compliant care food product line), and Hong Kong's 照護食 adoption work.

We publish dysphagia knowledge openly because the standard exists and the public deserves access. For our AI programme, visit [careez.org](https://careez.org).

## Our Mission

Every day, over **600,000 people** in Hong Kong live with dysphagia (swallowing difficulty). Between 30–40% of elderly care home residents require texture-modified diets to eat safely.

Yet caregivers and care home staff often lack systematic training resources.

**Our mission:** To provide every caregiver, care worker, and speech therapist with **free, reliable, and accessible** care food information — so that every elderly person can eat safely and with dignity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is softmeal.org?**
A: softmeal.org is Hong Kong's first free public knowledge platform for dysphagia care food. It provides IDDSI level guides, safe recipes, caregiver tools, and referral resources.

**Q: Who is softmeal.org for?**
A: Family caregivers, elderly care home staff, speech-language therapists, dietitians, and institutional kitchen teams managing residents with swallowing difficulties.

**Q: Is the information clinically validated?**
A: All content is reviewed by registered speech-language therapists and follows IDDSI international standards. The information is for educational reference — individualized assessment requires a qualified clinician.

**Q: How do I find a recipe for IDDSI Level 4?**
A: Use the recipe library at [softmeal.org/en/recipes/](/en/recipes/) and filter by IDDSI level. Each recipe includes a fork pressure test result to confirm texture compliance.

**Q: How is softmeal.org operated?**
A: The platform is operated by Carewells Limited with knowledge support from SeniorDeli (康樂齡). No supplier pays for inclusion or ranking in our directories.
