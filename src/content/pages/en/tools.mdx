## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are all these tools completely free?**
A: Yes. All tools above are available free of charge through the SeniorDeli app for non-commercial healthcare and educational use.

**Q: Can the tools be used offline?**
A: The SeniorDeli app supports offline functionality, making it suitable for care home frontline staff in environments with unreliable internet connectivity.

**Q: Is the EAT-10 questionnaire clinically validated?**
A: Yes. EAT-10 is a widely validated screening tool with documented sensitivity and specificity for identifying swallowing difficulties in elderly patients. See [Belafsky et al. 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19105604/).

**Q: Can these tools replace a speech therapist's assessment?**
A: No. These tools support documentation, screening, and education — they cannot replace a formal clinical swallowing assessment by a qualified speech-language therapist. IDDSI level assignment must always be confirmed by an SLP.

**Q: What languages does the app support?**
A: The SeniorDeli app supports Traditional Chinese and English, with selected tools also available in Simplified Chinese.
