# Free Digital Tools

Free tools for healthcare professionals, care home staff, and families managing dysphagia.

---

## EAT-10 Dysphagia Screening

Run a standardised EAT-10 swallowing assessment online — a validated 10-item questionnaire used by speech therapists worldwide to identify swallowing difficulties.

- Takes under 2 minutes
- Validated for use with elderly patients
- Results exportable for clinical records

[Try EAT-10 →](https://seniordeli.com/assessment)

---

## IDDSI Level Matcher

Answer 5 questions to find the right IDDSI texture level for your resident or patient. Based on the IDDSI framework used across Hong Kong hospitals and care homes.

- No sign-up required
- Covers Levels 0–7 (drinks and foods)
- Includes food preparation guidance

[Use IDDSI Matcher →](https://seniordeli.com/pilot)

---

## Download the SeniorDeli App (Free)

EAT-10 screening, IDDSI matching, meal logging, and daily caregiver tips — all in one app. Free for healthcare professionals.

- iOS & Android
- Works offline
- Suitable for care home staff daily use

[Get the App →](https://seniordeli.com/pilot)
