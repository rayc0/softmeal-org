**IDDSI Level 4 | 30 minutes | Medium**

## Ingredients (1 serving)

- 200ml smooth congee base (cooked until rice grains are fully dissolved)
- 50g chicken breast (steamed until fully cooked)
- 50ml clear chicken stock
- A small amount of cornstarch (if needed to adjust consistency)

## Method

1. Steam chicken breast until fully cooked and pull into small pieces
2. Blend chicken with stock until completely smooth (at least 2 minutes)
3. Add congee base and blend for another 30 seconds
4. Strain through a fine sieve to ensure no lumps
5. Heat on low until reaching a suitable serving temperature (not exceeding 65°C to avoid scalding)
6. If too thin, add a small amount of cornstarch slurry to thicken to Level 4

## Texture Test

**Fork pressure test:** Passes Level 4

## Notes

The congee base must be cooked until completely smooth with no visible rice grains. Any visible grains create a dual-texture that is unsafe for dysphagia patients.

## Nutrition

Chicken breast is an excellent low-fat protein source. Combined with the carbohydrates from the congee base, this provides a balanced meal.
