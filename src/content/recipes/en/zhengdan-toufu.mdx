**IDDSI Level 4 | 10 minutes | Easy**

## Ingredients (1 serving)

- 2 eggs
- 50g silken tofu
- 120ml clear chicken stock (unsalted or low-sodium)
- A few drops of light soy sauce (optional)

## Method

1. Mash silken tofu with a fork until very smooth
2. Beat eggs and gently mix with stock (do not over-beat)
3. Add tofu purée and strain through a fine sieve to remove air bubbles
4. Pour into a bowl and cover with cling film (leave a small gap)
5. Steam on high heat for 8 minutes until just cooked — do not overcook (surface should be smooth, not pitted)
6. Add a few drops of soy sauce or sesame oil to taste

## Texture Test

**Fork pressure test:** Passes Level 4 — food flattens completely under fork pressure and does not spring back

## Notes

Straining the egg mixture is essential for a smooth, even texture. The surface should be flat and glossy — a pitted surface indicates overcooked or poorly strained egg.

## Nutrition

Eggs provide high-quality protein; silken tofu provides plant-based protein and calcium. Ideal for elderly residents requiring a high-protein diet.
