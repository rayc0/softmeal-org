**IDDSI Level 4 | 20 minutes | Easy**

## Ingredients (2 servings)

- 200g pumpkin (peeled and deseeded)
- 3 tablespoons single cream (or full-fat milk as substitute)
- 1 teaspoon unsalted butter
- Pinch of white pepper

## Method

1. Cut pumpkin into small pieces and steam for 15 minutes until completely tender
2. While hot, blend to a very smooth purée
3. Add cream and butter, blend for another 10 seconds
4. Taste and adjust — add a little hot water or milk if too thick
5. Strain through a sieve to ensure even texture

## Texture Test

**Fork pressure test:** Passes Level 4 — smooth and uniform, completely flattens under fork pressure

## Nutrition

Pumpkin is rich in Beta-carotene and Vitamin A, beneficial for elderly vision. The cream provides additional calories, suitable for residents who are underweight.
