**IDDSI Level 4 | 10 minutes | Very Easy**

## Ingredients (1 serving)

- 1 ripe banana (best when skin has black spots)
- 3 tablespoons instant oats
- 150ml warm milk

## Method

1. Soak instant oats in warm milk for 5 minutes until soft
2. Slice the banana
3. Blend oats, milk, and banana together until completely smooth
4. Strain through a sieve to ensure no lumps
5. If too thick, add a little milk to adjust

## Texture Test

**Fork pressure test:** Passes Level 4 — uniform smooth texture, completely flattens under fork pressure

## Advantages

- No cooking required — quick to prepare
- Natural sweetness from banana — no added sugar needed
- Oats provide dietary fibre to aid digestion

## Notes

The banana must be fully ripe. Unripe bananas contain resistant starch that is harder to digest, and they lack sweetness.
