**IDDSI Level 5 | 20 minutes | Medium**

## Ingredients (1 serving)

- 60g fish paste (commercially prepared, boneless)
- 60g broccoli (florets only, no stems)
- 80ml clear chicken stock
- Small amount of cornstarch

## Method

1. Cut broccoli florets into small pieces (approximately 3mm), steam for 8 minutes until soft
2. Mix fish paste with a small amount of cornstarch and water, form into small balls (approximately 1cm diameter)
3. Bring chicken stock to a boil, add fish balls, cook until done (approximately 5 minutes)
4. Add broccoli pieces
5. Thicken slightly with cornstarch slurry to keep food moist
6. Ensure all pieces are under 4mm

## Texture Test

**Fork pressure test:** Passes Level 5 — fork side easily separates pieces; moist throughout

## Notes

Use only broccoli florets — stems are too firm for Level 5. Fish balls must not exceed 1cm diameter.

## Safety Note

⚠️ Always check for fish bones before serving. Fish bones are one of the highest-risk foods for dysphagia patients. Commercial fish paste should be bone-free, but always verify.
